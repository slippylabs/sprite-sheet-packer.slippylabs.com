SPRITE SHEET PACKER

Drop your frames in, or cut up a sheet you already have. This packs them tightly, trims the empty space around each one without moving where it draws, and hands you the atlas plus the JSON your engine wants.

Drop PNG frames here, or

Frames are ordered by filename, so run_01.png … run_08.png comes out in the right order.

Cells are read left to right, top to bottom. Fully transparent cells are skipped.

Hover a frame to see its rectangle.
